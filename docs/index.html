What is Dragon Boat Replay?

Dragon Boat Replay is a free, open-source Garmin FIT map viewer focused on water sports. Garmin watches and bike computers record activities as FIT files. Those files are great for training history, but hard to share as a living map. This site turns a FIT (or the ZIP you download from Garmin Connect) into a browser-based GPS track replay with:

  • Satellite basemap of your full route and animated boat marker
  • Speed-over-time chart synced to the map
  • Stroke rate (cadence) and distance readouts where the FIT includes them
  • One-click export of a portable HTML replay and a social “poster” image

Unlike many cloud training platforms, processing stays on your device. That matters when you care about GPS privacy for home water, training venues, or personal routes.

How it works

  1. Export your activity from Garmin Connect as original FIT, or download the activity package ZIP that contains the FIT.
  2. Upload on this page (or try the demo). The browser parses FIT records with a client-side library — no upload to our servers.
  3. Replay on the map: play/pause, change speed, scrub the slider, click the chart, or use keyboard shortcuts.
  4. Share by downloading HTML for email/Drive, or a poster PNG with a ready caption for Instagram, Stories, or group chats.

Technical note: GPS coordinates in FIT are converted from semicircles to degrees; timestamps are shown in Hong Kong Time (HKT, UTC+8) for local paddling clubs. Sampling is whatever your device recorded (often irregular, not fixed 1 Hz).

Keyboard shortcuts (during replay)

  • Space — play / pause (resumes from the same place you stopped)
  • ← → — step one GPS sample left or right
  • 1–5 — playback rate 0.25×, 0.5×, 1×, 2×, 4×

How to export a FIT file from Garmin Connect

To use this free FIT file map viewer, you need the original activity data from Garmin:

  1. Sign in to Garmin Connect on the web.
  2. Open the activity you want (dragon boat, SUP, paddling, or similar).
  3. Use the gear / export menu and choose Export Original (FIT) or download the activity export ZIP.
  4. Return here and drop the .fit or .zip on the upload box above.

If the file has no GPS positions (indoor or sensor-only activity), the tool will explain that no map points were found. Outdoor water sessions with GPS enabled work best.

Who this is for

Built first for dragon boat crews and recreational paddlers who record sessions on Garmin devices, including athletes in Hong Kong and other harbour / open-water clubs. It also works well for:

  • Stand-up paddleboard (SUP) and outrigger training reviews
  • Coaches sharing a route replay in a team chat without requiring Garmin accounts for everyone
  • Athletes who want a simple “what did my piece look like?” map without a full training SaaS
  • Anyone who needs a private, offline-friendly HTML copy of a track

The synthetic demo uses a fictional mid-harbour paddling loop so you can test the player without uploading personal GPS data.

Privacy policy (summary)

Your activity files are processed only in your browser. This static site does not upload your FIT or ZIP to a server we control. GPS coordinates stay on your device unless you choose to download and share the generated HTML or poster yourself.

Map tiles and open-source libraries load from public CDNs (for example Leaflet and imagery providers) as needed to draw the map. We do not run user accounts, analytics beacons for ad profiling, or a backend database of routes on this Pages deployment. If third-party ads (such as Google AdSense) are added in the future, this page will be updated to disclose any additional cookies or data practices.

Open-source code is published on GitHub so you can inspect exactly how parsing and replay work.

Frequently asked questions

Is Dragon Boat Replay free?

Yes. The web app is free to use. The project is open source under the repository terms on GitHub.

Does my GPS get uploaded to the internet?

Not to our servers. Parsing runs locally in JavaScript. You only leave the page’s origin if you share a file you downloaded or if the map requests public tile images.

What file types are supported?

Garmin .fit activity files and .zip archives that contain a FIT (typical Garmin Connect export). GPX/TCX are not the primary path today.

Can I use this for sports other than dragon boat?

Yes. Any outdoor FIT with latitude/longitude records can replay. Naming and defaults lean paddling, but cycling or running tracks will still map if GPS is present.

Why is stroke rate missing sometimes?

Stroke rate comes from cadence fields in the FIT. If your device or activity profile did not record cadence, the replay still shows map, speed, and distance.
